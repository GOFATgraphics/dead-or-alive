FAQ

Billing questions

Why do I need an account to buy?

So your purchase lands somewhere. You create a free account, then pay on Polar with the same email, and the payment is matched to your dashboard automatically. Nothing is emailed to you later; it's all in the dashboard.

Who handles the payment?

Polar, our merchant of record. It takes the payment, handles tax, and sends your receipt. We never see your card.

How do I cancel Stay Alive?

From your dashboard, any time. It stays on until the end of the month you paid for, then stops. No more charges.

Is the free stamp still free?

Yes. No account, no card. The account is only needed to save stamps or to buy a plan.

What about my test account for the app review?

It must be a throwaway test account, never your main account, with no real data and no saved card. We encrypt it, use it only for that review, and delete it after. You can revoke it from your dashboard at any time.

Refunds?

If the Kill Sheet didn't run on your page, or Stay Alive never switched on, write to us and we refund it through Polar.
