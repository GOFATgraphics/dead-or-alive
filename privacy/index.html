Privacy

What we keep, and why

Last updated October 1, 2026

What you give us

The page URL and the email address you type into the form. We use them for one thing: to look at that page's first screen and send you the result.

Payment

Checkout runs on Polar, which handles the payment as merchant of record. We never see your card number. Polar shares your order with us: your email, the page URL, the product, and the amount.

What we make

A screenshot of your page's first screen, our circles on it, one sentence, and the stamp. We publish it at an unlisted link and email you that link. Anyone who has the link can open it. It is not listed anywhere, and we ask search engines not to index it.

Who processes it

  • Polar: payment and order details.
  • Vercel: hosts this site and stores the published stamps.
  • Resend: sends the email with your link.
  • Upstash: stores the anonymous visit counts described below.

Visit counts

We count visits and clicks on this site to see what works: which page and button, the country your connection comes from, phone or computer, and the site that sent you. We do it on our own server, without cookies and without storing your IP address. Each day's visits are counted with a code made from your connection and browser that changes every day, so we can't recognize you from one day to the next or link a visit to your email. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, we don't count you at all. Counts are deleted after about a year.

What we don't do

No ads, no third-party trackers, no cookies. We don't sell or share your email, and we don't add you to a mailing list.

Deleting it

We keep your order and stamp until you ask us to remove them. To take your stamp down or delete your data, contact us at %CONTACT%. We will confirm when it's done.
