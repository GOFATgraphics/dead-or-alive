Privacy

What we keep, and why

Last updated October 26, 2026

Who we are

Stamp My Page is run by Gofat Labs, which decides how the data on this page is used and is responsible for it. "We" and "us" mean Gofat Labs.

What you give us

The page URL (your site, app, or store page) you type into the form. We use it for one thing: to look at that page's first screen and show you the result. We don't ask for your name, your email, or a card.

How we read your page

When you press the button, a screenshot service opens your page logged out and captures the first screen, and reads its main headline and button labels. Our server also loads the page once to time it and read its public tags (title, description, share preview) and what it's built and hosted on. Fixed rules score what we found and pick the stamp and the sentence. No AI reads your page for the free stamp. If the page can't be captured, it retries once; if it still can't, nothing is published and the page tells you so.

If you buy the Kill Sheet or Stay Alive

Checkout runs on Polar, which handles the payment as merchant of record. We never see your card number. Polar shares your order with us: your email, the page URL, the product, and the amount. We use your email only to deliver what you bought.

What we make

A screenshot of your page's first screen, the stamp, one sentence, the score and the checks behind it, a share card, and the stack and speed check. We publish it at an unlisted link and open it for you. Anyone who has the link can open it. The stamp itself is not listed anywhere, and we ask search engines not to index it. If your page is stamped ALIVE or COPE, its domain, verdict, and score may appear in the "Latest stamps" list on our homepage; the stamp itself and its link do not. DEAD stamps are never listed. Ask us and we remove yours.

Who processes it

  • Vercel: hosts this site and stores the screenshots, drafts, and published stamps.
  • Microlink: opens your page and takes the first-screen screenshot.
  • Polar: payment and order details, only if you buy something.
  • Resend: sends what you bought by email, only if you buy something.
  • Upstash: stores the anonymous visit counts and daily stamp limits described below.

Visit counts

We count visits and clicks on this site to see what works: which page and button, the country your connection comes from, phone or computer, and the site that sent you. We do it on our own server, without cookies and without storing your IP address. Each day's visits are counted with a code made from your connection and browser that changes every day, so we can't recognize you from one day to the next. The same kind of daily code counts how many free stamps each visitor has used today, so nobody can use them all up. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, we don't count you at all. Counts are deleted after about a year.

What we don't do

No ads, no third-party trackers, no cookies. We don't sell or share anything you give us, and there's no mailing list to land on.

Deleting it

We keep your stamp until you ask us to remove it. To take your stamp down or delete your data, contact us at %CONTACT%. We will confirm when it's done.
