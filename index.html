Questions

Anything else: hello@stampmypage.com

Is a human involved?
No. Capture, analysis, and the stamp card are fully automated. Your result opens on screen the moment it’s ready.
How fast is it?
About 60 seconds from payment to stamp card. The results page waits with you and opens the stamp as soon as it’s done.
What if it fails?
It retries automatically. If the page still can’t be captured, for example because it’s down or blocks bots, your $1 is refunded automatically.
Does it work for apps?
Yes. Paste an App Store or Google Play link and the stamp judges the store listing, which is the first screen people see before they install.
