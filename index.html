The three stamps

One word for your first screen.

  1. DEAD

    The offer is missing. Nobody can say what you sell, or who it's for, from the first screen.

  2. COPE

    It looks real, but it isn't clear. The product, the buyer, or the reason to pay takes decoding.

  3. ALIVE

    It lands. A first-time visitor can name the product, the buyer, and why they'd pay, before scrolling.
