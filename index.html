Why pages fail

7 reasons your landing page isn’t converting

A stranger decides in about five seconds. These are the things that make them leave, and every one of them is a check in your stamp.

  1. The headline doesn’t say what it is

    If the biggest line on the page could describe any product, visitors can’t picture yours. Name the thing and the result.

  2. It’s written in pitch-deck

    “Reimagine”, “unlock”, “seamless”, “AI-powered”. They sound big and say nothing. Plain words convert.

  3. It doesn’t say who it’s for

    People buy when they recognise themselves. “For sales teams on Zoom” beats “for modern teams”.

  4. There’s no clear next step

    A visitor who’s interested needs one button with a verb and a payoff: “Try it free”, “Get the template”.

  5. Too many buttons fight

    Get started, Book a demo, Watch the film, Join the waitlist. Four choices feel like none. Keep one main action.

  6. It loads too slowly

    Every second before the first screen shows is a second to hit Back. Aim for the page to arrive in under 2.5 seconds.

  7. It breaks on a phone

    Most launch traffic is mobile. A missing viewport tag turns your page into a shrunken desktop site.

  8. Plus 6 more

    Headline length, https, page weight, tab title, search description and share preview. 13 checks in all.

FAQ

Questions

Why is my landing page not converting?

Most pages lose visitors in the first five seconds: the headline doesn’t say what the product is, it doesn’t say who it’s for, there’s no single clear next step, or the page is slow or broken on a phone. The stamp checks all of these on your first screen and tells you which ones fail.

Is it really free?

Yes. The stamp needs no card and no account. Stamp as many pages as you like. A free account is optional: it saves your stamps in one place.

What do the Kill Sheet and Stay Alive include?

The Kill Sheet ($19, one-time) runs the moment you pay: a scout of your whole page, your headline, subhead and button rewritten, 3 to 5 fixes ranked by impact, a free re-stamp, and one app review. Stay Alive ($20 a month) switches monitoring on the moment you subscribe: a fresh stamp every week, daily uptime and speed checks, and one app review a month. Both live in your dashboard.

How does the app review work?

From your dashboard you send your site or app link, what to test, and, if it needs a login, a throwaway test account. We test the logged-out first screen, signup and onboarding, and the core flow, back to back. The result lands in your dashboard with a status you can follow: Received, In review, Delivered. The test account is deleted after the review.

What do DEAD, COPE and ALIVE mean?

Your first screen is scored on 13 weighted checks. 80 or more with a plain-English headline is ALIVE, 55 or more is COPE, and below that, or no headline at all, is DEAD. The same rules for every page.

Does it work with sites built on Lovable, Bolt, v0 or Replit?

Yes. Apps built with AI app builders like Lovable, Bolt, v0, Replit, Cursor and Windsurf draw their page in the browser, so the stamp reads the page the way a visitor sees it, not just its raw HTML. It also tells you which builder it spotted.

Does it work with Framer, Webflow, Shopify or WordPress?

Yes, any public web page: Framer, Webflow, Shopify, WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Next.js, Carrd, Notion sites and more. App Store and Google Play listings aren’t supported yet; send your app’s website.

What if it can’t stamp my page?

It retries once by itself. If your page still can’t be captured, usually because it’s down, blocks bots, or sits behind a login, it tells you on the spot.

Who can see my stamp?

Your full stamp is only seen by people you give the link to, and search engines are asked not to index it. ALIVE and COPE pages may show up by domain, verdict and score in the Latest stamps list on this page. DEAD never does. Ask and we take yours off.
