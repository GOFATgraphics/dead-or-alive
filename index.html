Questions

Anything else: hello@stampmypage.com

Is it really free?
Yes. No card, no account, no trial that turns into a bill. There’s a daily limit per person so the stamp keeps working for everyone.
Who decides the stamp?
Fixed rules, not a mood. The same 13 checks for every page, each with a weight. Score 80+ with a clear headline is ALIVE, 55+ is COPE, the rest is DEAD.
What if it fails?
It retries once by itself. If your page still can’t be captured, usually because it’s down, blocks bots, or sits behind a login, we tell you on the spot.
What comes after the stamp?
Your result shows every check that failed and how to fix it. If you’d rather we fix it, or keep watching it for you, the options are right there under your stamp.
Does it work for apps?
Send your app’s website. Store listings can’t be stamped yet.
Who sees my stamp?
Only people you give the link to. It isn’t listed anywhere, and search engines are asked not to index it.
