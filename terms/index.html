Who we are

Stamp My Page is run by Gofat Labs. In these terms, "we" and "us" mean Gofat Labs, and the agreement for your stamp is between you and Gofat Labs.

The stamp

One verdict on the first screen of one page (a website, web app, or SaaS homepage): a screenshot, one sentence, a score out of 100 with the checks behind it, and DEAD, COPE, or ALIVE, plus a short stack and speed check. Fixed rules make it automatically from your first screen and the page's HTML; no AI is involved. It is our opinion of whether a new visitor gets your offer before scrolling. It is not an audit, and it does not promise any result for your business.

Price

Free. No card, no account. We may add a daily limit, or change or stop the free stamp, at any time.

Timing

The stamp arrives on screen in about a minute. If the page can't be captured, it retries once automatically. If it still can't, for example because the page doesn't load or sits behind a login (we judge what a logged-out visitor sees), we tell you and publish nothing.

Kill Sheet and Stay Alive

Optional paid follow-ups: the Kill Sheet ($19, one-time) and Stay Alive ($20 a month, cancel anytime). They're sold through Polar, the merchant of record, so its checkout terms cover the payment itself. What each includes is listed on the homepage and your result page.

Your page

Send pages you are allowed to share. Don't send pages behind a login or pages that show private information.

Your result link

Your stamp lives at an unlisted link. Anyone you share it with can see it. An ALIVE or COPE page may be shown by domain, verdict, and score in the "Latest stamps" list on our homepage; DEAD pages never are. Ask us at any time and we take it down or off the list.
