Who we are

Stamp My Page is run by Gofat Labs. In these terms, "we" and "us" mean Gofat Labs, and the agreement for your stamp is between you and Gofat Labs.

The stamp

One verdict on the first screen of one page (a website, web app, or SaaS homepage): a screenshot, one sentence, a score out of 100 with the checks behind it, and DEAD, COPE, or ALIVE, plus a short stack and speed check. Fixed rules make it automatically from your first screen and the page's HTML; no AI is involved. It is our opinion of whether a new visitor gets your offer before scrolling. It is not an audit, and it does not promise any result for your business.

Price

Free. No card, no account. A free account is optional and only saves your stamps. We may add a daily limit, or change or stop the free stamp, at any time.

Timing

The stamp arrives on screen in about a minute. If the page can't be captured, it retries once automatically. If it still can't, for example because the page doesn't load or sits behind a login (we judge what a logged-out visitor sees), we tell you and publish nothing.

Kill Sheet and Stay Alive

Two optional paid plans, sold through our payment provider, which acts as merchant of record, so its checkout terms cover the payment itself. You create a free account first and pay with the same email; the purchase is matched to that account. Everything is delivered in your dashboard.

  • Kill Sheet, $19 one-time. Instant, the moment the payment is confirmed: dashboard access and an automated report on the page you name, with a scout of the whole page, your headline, subhead, and button rewritten, and 3 to 5 fixes ranked by impact, ready in about a minute. Also included: one free re-stamp that runs the report again when you press it, and one app review.
  • Stay Alive, $20 a month, or $200 a year. Instant, the moment the subscription is confirmed: dashboard access and monitoring switched on, with the first uptime check and re-stamp running right away. After that: a re-stamp every week, an uptime and speed check about every 5 minutes (a failed check is confirmed with a second one before it counts), an email if the page goes down and again when it is back, and an alert when its tools, tracking pixels, speed, or verdict change between re-stamps. It covers one site. Members can add more sites from the dashboard for $5 a month each ($50 a year each on yearly billing), charged pro rata for the rest of the period; extra sites are only sold on top of an active Stay Alive plan. Also included: one app review a month in total, not per site, for the site the member picks. Cancel anytime from your dashboard; it stays on until the end of the month you paid for, then stops with no further charges.

The automated parts are rules, not people, and not AI. They are our opinion of your page and don't promise any result for your business.

App reviews

An app review is a benefit of an active Kill Sheet or Stay Alive membership, requested by you inside the dashboard. A person tests what you ask, end to end: the logged-out first screen, signup and onboarding, and the core flow, back to back. The result (problems found, fixes ranked, rewritten copy) is posted to your dashboard, and you can follow its status there: Received, In review, Delivered.

If testing needs a login, you may give us a test account. It must be a throwaway test account, never your main account, with no real data and no saved card. We encrypt it, use it only for that review, and delete it after, and you can revoke it anytime from your dashboard. Don't send credentials for a production account, an account with real customer data, or anything with a saved payment method; if you do, we delete them and ask for a test account instead.

Refunds

If the Kill Sheet's report never ran on your page, or Stay Alive's monitoring never switched on, write to us and we refund it.

Your page

Send pages you are allowed to share. Don't send pages behind a login or pages that show private information.

Your result link

Your stamp lives at an unlisted link. Anyone you share it with can see it. An ALIVE or COPE page may be shown by domain, verdict, and score in the "Latest stamps" list on our homepage; DEAD pages never are. Ask us at any time and we take it down or off the list.
